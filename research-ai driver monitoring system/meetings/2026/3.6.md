# **1. System Performance**
### **Asynchronous Inference Engine**

- **The Problem**: UI "stuttering" during heavy AI model calculations.
- **The Solution**: Decoupled AI models into a background `Worker` thread.
- **Result**:
    - Smooth **30 FPS** camera feed.
    - Zero UI lag during Heart Rate and Fatigue analysis.
    - Improved thread safety using `Queue` and `threading`.

# **2. Physiological Robustness**
### **Filtering the Noise**

- **Signal Quality Index (SQI)**:
    - Real-time FFT analysis to calculate **SNR (Signal-to-Noise Ratio)**.

- **Hybrid SpO2 Logic**:
    - Blends AI Deep Learning with traditional analytical formulas.
