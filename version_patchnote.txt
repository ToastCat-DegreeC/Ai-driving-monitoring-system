# AI Driving Monitoring System - Version Patch Notes

### Decision Engine & Multi-Head Attention Fusion (Part C)
- **Retrained MHA-BiLSTM Weights (`models/fusion_model.h5`)**: Resolved inverted PERCLOS correlation in pilot training data. The 4-head MHA-BiLSTM network now properly predicts ~12% (Alert), ~65% (Fatigued/Yawning), and >90% (Critical on eye closure >= 1.5s).
- **Interpretability & XAI Attention Heads**: Attention weights now legitimately focus on closed-eye frames during micro-sleeps and blinks, achieving Validation MAE of **0.0401**.

- **Resolution Upscaling**: Automatically upscaled camera feed to minimum 720p height with 340px telemetry panel; enabled resizable/maximizable window (`cv2.WINDOW_NORMAL`).
- **Annotated Attention Bar (XAI)**: Added timeline bounds (`-2.0s (Past)` to `Now (0s)`), `[MHA XAI]` badge, and dynamic amber/orange peak highlight for anomaly focus.
- **Live Ocular State Indicator**: Added real-time `OCULAR STATE: OPEN (Green)` vs `CLOSED (Red)` status badge.
- **Startup Warm-up Gating**: Suppressed initial 99% probability spike by cleanly gating predictions during the 2.0-second calibration window.

## [Version 1.3] - 2026-03-06

### System Performance & Architecture
- **Asynchronous Inference Engine**: Migrated heavy AI models (HR, SpO2, PERCLOS, Fusion) to a dedicated background `AIBackgroundWorker` thread using `threading` and `Queue`.
- **UI Responsiveness**: Decoupled AI processing from the camera loop, ensuring a consistent **30 FPS** video feed and eliminating "stuttering" during batch model execution.
- **Centralized Configuration**: Created `config.py` to house all hyperparameters, thresholds, and model paths, significantly improving project maintainability and tuning speed.

### Physiological Robustness & Vitals
- **Signal Quality Index (SQI)**: Implemented an FFT-based Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) monitor for rPPG. The system now enters a "Searching..." state if signal noise (e.g., from bad lighting) exceeds the `SQI_THRESHOLD`.
- **Hybrid SpO2 Logic**: Developed a robust estimation strategy blending AI model predictions with calibrated "Ratio-of-Ratios" analytical formulas.
- **Advanced Smoothing**: Integrated heavy EMA (Exponential Moving Average) filters for SpO2 and Heart Rate, resulting in stable, realistic vitals (stabilizing SpO2 from erratic 80% to a healthy 95-98% range).

### Tracking & Visualization
- **Precise Head Pose Tracking**: Re-mapped 3D-to-2D landmarks to resolve axis flipping and applied EMA smoothing to eliminate rotational jitter.
- **3D Axis Visualization**: Added a real-time RGB coordinate axis projected from the nose, providing instant visual feedback for head Pitch, Yaw, and Roll.
- **Interactive Fatigue Logic**: Integrated physical heuristics (yawning and long eye closure) into the Fusion Model decision engine for immediate "CRITICAL" fatigue responses.

### User Interface (UI/UX)
- **Professional Side-Panel Dashboard**: Replaced basic OpenCV text overlays with a modern, dark-themed dashboard.
- **Real-Time Pulse Wave**: Added a scrolling "ECG-style" graph to visualize the raw rPPG pulse signal.
- **Dynamic Fatigue Meter**: Implemented a color-coded probability bar (Green -> Yellow -> Red) for intuitive risk assessment.
- **Compact Layout**: Optimized the dashboard design to remain functional and readable on standard webcam resolutions (480p/720p).

### Validation & Benchmarking
- **Automated Benchmarking Suite**: Developed `benchmark_rppg.py` to validate system accuracy against the **UBFC-rPPG** dataset.
- **Performance Baseline**: Verified an average Heart Rate MAE of **6.03 BPM** across multiple subjects.

### TODO List (Next Steps)

## [Version 1.0] - 2026-01-14

### Core Features & Integration
- **Full Model Integration**: Successfully integrated the 1D-CNN Heart Rate (HR) model, the PERCLOS drowsiness model, and the Eye CNN classifier into the main real-time system (`main.py`).
- **Hybrid Eye State Logic**: Implemented a robust dual-check system combining geometric EAR (Eye Aspect Ratio) and appearance-based CNN inference. Added temporal smoothing (Exponential Moving Average) to eliminate "flickering" between open/closed states.
- **Heart Rate Stabilization**: Developed a multi-stage HR filter including:
    - **Valid Range Filter**: Restricts readings to 45-160 BPM.
    - **Jump Rejection**: Automatically ignores sudden spikes (>12 BPM) caused by motion or lighting changes.
    - **Smoothing**: Uses a moving average to provide a steady, readable pulse value.

### Performance & Robustness
- **High-Sensitivity Tracking**: Optimized MediaPipe Face Mesh thresholds to allow face detection even while wearing masks or bandanas.
- **FPS Synchronization**: Enforced a 30Hz sampling rate for physiological signals (rPPG/EAR) to match training conditions, even when the camera runs at 60 FPS.
- **Loop Optimization**: Implemented frame-skipping for heavy AI models (Eye CNN runs every 5 frames, HR/PERCLOS every 30 frames) to maintain a smooth 60 FPS UI experience.
- **Dynamic ROIs**: Updated forehead and cheek regions to scale dynamically based on face size and orientation.

### Image Processing & AI
- **Contrast Enhancement**: Integrated Histogram Equalization for eye crops to improve detection accuracy in low-light or high-shadow conditions.
- **Synthetic PERCLOS Training**: Generated a synthetic time-series dataset to train the PERCLOS 1D-CNN model in the absence of a localized DMD video dataset.
- **Input Validation**: Fixed several "AttributeErrors" and "Reshape Errors" by enforcing strict NumPy array casting across the pipeline.

### Debugging & Visualization (Developer Tools)
- **Live Debug Overlays**: Added real-time visualization of the 468-point face mesh, forehead/cheek ROI boxes, and an FPS counter.
- **Eye-In-The-Box**: Added persistent display of the actual eye crops being fed to the CNN for live alignment verification.
- **Raw Metric Stream**: Displayed raw P (Probability) and EAR (Geometry) values on the HUD for fine-tuning.
